“No. Show me their **Harishare pipeline**, **ADG Ready-for-sale**, and **FML pending-invoice command** in one login. That’s the gap.”

“Start with one module: Farm Finance + Crop Sales, or FMD Sales, or FML Clients. Land and expand — not big-bang ERP.”
